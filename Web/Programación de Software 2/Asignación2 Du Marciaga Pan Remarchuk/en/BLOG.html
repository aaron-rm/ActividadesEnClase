Healthy omelet

This healthy omelet is an excellent choice for a nutritious breakfast. It contains protein, vitamins, and healthy fats that help maintain good energy throughout the day.

Ingredients

  • 2 eggs
  • Spinach
  • Tomato
  • Low-fat cheese
  • Salt to taste

Preparation

  1. Beat the eggs in a bowl.
  2. Add salt and mix well.
  3. Pour into a hot skillet.
  4. Add the toppings (spinach, tomato, cheese).
  5. Cook until cooked through.

Nutritional Information

Calories Protein Fat Carbohydrates
250 kcal 18 g 15 g 5 g
